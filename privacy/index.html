Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Anvil Tech LLC ("Anvil Tech", "we", "us") handles when you play our games, including Candy Unclip, on the web (including on Playgama and itch.io) and on TikTok, and when you visit this website. Our games are made to need as little information as possible: you never have to create an account or give us your name, email address or phone number to play.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (for example the level you have reached, the hints you have left, and your music, sound and vibration settings) is saved on your own device, in your browser's local storage or in the storage the platform gives the game. On Playgama, the platform may also keep it with your Playgama account if you are signed in there. It is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data or the platform's data for the game.

Gameplay statistics

On TikTok and on Playgama, the game reports anonymous gameplay events to the platform's analytics service (TikTok's on TikTok, Playgama's on Playgama), such as a level being started, cleared or failed and a hint being used. We use these figures to tune the difficulty of our levels. They do not include your name, contact details or the content of your account. Elsewhere on the web, including on itch.io, our games do not send gameplay statistics anywhere.

Signing in on TikTok

TikTok requires mini games to perform a "silent login" when they start. It gives the game a temporary code and shows no prompt. We do not exchange that code for your TikTok identity, and we do not receive your TikTok username, profile, followers or other account details. If this ever changes, we will update this policy before it does.

Advertising

Some versions of our games show ads served by the platform: rewarded video and interstitial ads by TikTok on TikTok, and by Playgama and its advertising partners on Playgama. These ad services may collect and use information about your device and your interaction with ads under their own policies. We do not receive personal information from them. See TikTok's Privacy Policy and Playgama's Privacy Policy. The version on itch.io shows no ads.

The platforms

When you play on TikTok, Playgama or itch.io, those companies process information about your use of their services under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and to operate the service, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The site itself uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we keep them only as long as we need to for that and for our records.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with anyone, except where the law requires us to.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how it is used. Because our games do not collect personal information, we usually hold none apart from emails you have sent us. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

Where we are

Anvil Tech LLC is a company organized in the United States.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
