Candy Unclip

A free puzzle game by Anvil Tech LLC

The game

Twist the candy rings until each gap lines up with its clip, and watch them slip free. A ring pinned under another won't budge, so work out the order. Free a ring that carries a key and its padlock springs open. Thirty hand-picked levels lead into endless generated ones, every one checked to be solvable. Stuck? A hint shows which ring to turn and which way.

How to play

  • Drag a ring with your finger or the mouse to rotate it.
  • When its gap lines up with the clip holding it, the ring slips out.
  • Rings pinned under others won't turn until those are free.
  • Free every ring to clear the level. Each level gives you three hearts for wrong turns.

Features

  • 30 hand-picked levels, then endless generated ones; a solver checks every level can be solved.
  • Hints that show which ring to turn and which way.
  • Plays in portrait on phones and in the browser on desktop.
  • No account. Your progress is saved on your device.
  • Music and sound effects, each with its own switch.
